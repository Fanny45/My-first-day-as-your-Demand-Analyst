I’ve already planned demand, end to end

At Terra Nostra Organics, a wholesale trader of organic foods, I ran the full loop from customer order to supplier contract. In an agent-assisted team, each step gets faster.

  1. Receive the customer purchase order

    I read quantities, delivery windows and terms for each customer.With an agent: orders are parsed and checked automatically.

  2. Enter it in the system

    I recorded every order in the ERP (Dynamics 365 Business Central).With an agent: entry is drafted, I validate exceptions.

  3. Pull ERP data with JetReports in Excel

    I built the report that showed which quantity was needed in which month.With an agent: the monthly demand view refreshes itself.

  4. Match the best supplier to each customer

    I compared suppliers by fit, capacity and delivery reliability.With an agent: suppliers are scored, I choose.

  5. Negotiate price and purchase terms

    I negotiated prices and terms, and negotiated freight volumes with forwarders to keep a steady rate and avoid fuel surcharges.With an agent: benchmarks are ready before the call.

  6. Write the supplier contract with dated orders

    Each purchase order had the date we needed it, so demand was covered on time.With an agent: date risks are flagged early.
